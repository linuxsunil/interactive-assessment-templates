# Sales Objection Handler

A complete, working example of the quiz template applied to sales training.

## What This Quiz Does

6 realistic sales scenarios where you practice handling real-world objections:

1. **Price Objection** - Competitor costs less, why pay more?
2. **Feature Gap** - We don't have a feature they want
3. **Trust & Proof** - Worried about our long-term viability
4. **Timing Delay** - They want to wait until next quarter
5. **Comparison Pressure** - Comparing us against 3 competitors
6. **Recovery Play** - Competitor already impressed them

Each scenario has 4 response options with weighted scoring (0-3 points) and coaching feedback.

## How to Use This Example

### To Try It
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Take the quiz and see how you score!
3. Review your responses and the coaching feedback

### To Customize It
1. Open `quiz-data.json` in a text editor
2. Edit scenarios, options, and feedback to match YOUR product/service
3. Refresh browser to see changes immediately

### To Deploy to Rise 360
1. Copy all code from `index.html`
2. In Rise 360:
   - Click **Add Content Block**
   - Select **Web Object**
   - Choose **Custom HTML**
   - Paste code and save
3. Done! Your quiz is live in Rise

### To Use as a Template for Your Own Quiz
1. Copy this folder: `cp -r sales-objection-handler my-sales-quiz`
2. Edit `quiz-data.json` with your scenarios
3. Test in browser
4. Deploy!

## Customize For Your Product

Edit these in `quiz-data.json`:

- **Objection types** - Change to objections YOUR team actually faces
- **Product/service name** - Replace with your company/product
- **Specific features** - Match your product capabilities
- **Customer segments** - Use your target customer types
- **Pricing structure** - Reference your actual pricing model
