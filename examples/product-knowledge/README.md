# Product Knowledge Assessment

Test your ability to match customer problems to the right product features and solutions.

## What This Quiz Tests

5 product knowledge scenarios:

1. **Real-Time Inventory Sync** - Multi-location inventory tracking
2. **Custom Reporting** - Building flexible reports
3. **Multi-Channel Sales** - Omnichannel commerce platform
4. **Integration Challenge** - Connecting to other systems
5. **Team Collaboration** - Remote team workflows

Each scenario presents a customer problem. You must identify the right product feature or solution.

## How to Use

### To Try It
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Take the quiz
3. See how well you know your products!

### To Customize for Your Products
1. Open `quiz-data.json` in text editor
2. Replace scenarios with YOUR customer use cases
3. Update products/features to match YOUR offerings
4. Test and deploy to Rise 360

### To Deploy to Rise 360
1. Copy all code from `index.html`
2. In Rise 360:
   - Click **Add Content Block**
   - Select **Web Object**
   - Choose **Custom HTML**
   - Paste code and save
3. Done!

## Mastery Levels

- **85%+**: Product Expert - Deeply understands products, closes deals
- **70%+**: Strong Product Knowledge - Knows most products, needs review
- **50%+**: Building Knowledge - Learning products, needs training
- **<50%**: Product Training Needed - Review documentation with manager

## Why Product Knowledge Matters

- **Closing deals** - You can't sell what you don't understand
- **Customer satisfaction** - Right solution = happy customers
- **Avoiding overselling** - Recommend the right product, not the expensive one
- **Team efficiency** - Fewer support issues when recommendations are correct

## Tips for Success

- Know your product's key features and use cases
- Understand what problems each feature solves
- Be able to match customer needs to solutions
- Keep current on new features and capabilities
- Ask your product team when you're unsure
