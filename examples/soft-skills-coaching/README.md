# Manager Communication & Coaching Skills

Practice handling difficult conversations with employees - a core management skill.

## What This Quiz Tests

4 critical management scenarios:

1. **Giving Difficult Feedback** - When your feedback lands the wrong way
2. **Handling Conflict** - When team members clash
3. **Performance Decline** - When a good performer stumbles
4. **Delegating Stretch Work** - When you push someone to grow

Each scenario emphasizes:
- Listening first, judging later
- Empathy AND directness
- Curiosity over assumptions
- Coaching up, not just correcting

## How to Use

### To Try It
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Take the quiz
3. Reflect on your management style

### To Customize for Your Organization
1. Open `quiz-data.json` in text editor
2. Update scenarios with situations YOUR managers face
3. Adjust coaching feedback to match YOUR culture
4. Deploy to Rise 360

### To Deploy to Rise 360
1. Copy all code from `index.html`
2. In Rise 360:
   - Click **Add Content Block**
   - Select **Web Object**
   - Choose **Custom HTML**
   - Paste code and save
3. Done!

## Mastery Levels

- **85%+**: Exceptional Manager - Empathy + wisdom + clarity
- **70%+**: Strong Communicator - Handles most conversations well
- **50%+**: Developing Leadership - Building skills, needs practice
- **<50%**: Development Recommended - Consider management training

## Why Soft Skills Matter

Great managers:
- **Retain talent** - People stay because they trust you
- **Develop people** - Your team grows and gets promoted
- **Reduce turnover** - Good communication prevents exit conversations
- **Build culture** - Your team's engagement reflects your leadership
- **Get results** - Motivated people deliver better work

## Key Management Principles

From this quiz:
1. **Listen first** - Understand before you judge
2. **Validate feelings** - You can disagree with emotions
3. **Show intent** - Explain what you meant vs. how it landed
4. **Lead with curiosity** - Ask "What's going on?" not "Why did you mess up?"
5. **Coach up** - Help people grow, don't just correct mistakes
