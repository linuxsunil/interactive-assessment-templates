# Workplace Compliance Training

Learn to recognize and respond to workplace compliance issues correctly.

## What This Quiz Covers

4 critical compliance scenarios:

1. **Harassment Recognition** - Is something harassment?
2. **Reporting Procedure** - How to report compliance issues
3. **Investigation Process** - What to do during HR investigations
4. **Prevention Responsibility** - Your role in preventing discrimination

This quiz emphasizes that our policies require:
- Zero tolerance for harassment and discrimination
- Prompt reporting of violations
- Honest participation in investigations
- Everyone's responsibility to maintain a safe workplace

## How to Use

### To Try It
1. Open `index.html` in your browser
2. Take the quiz
3. Review the coaching feedback

### To Customize for Your Organization
1. Open `quiz-data.json` in text editor
2. Update company policies to match YOUR handbook
3. Change scenario details to YOUR situations
4. Test and deploy to Rise 360

### Key Customizations
- Update policy references to your company's specific policies
- Change scenarios to real situations your team faces
- Adjust mastery level descriptions to match your culture
- Add more scenarios if needed

### To Deploy to Rise 360
1. Copy all code from `index.html`
2. In Rise 360:
   - Click **Add Content Block**
   - Select **Web Object**
   - Choose **Custom HTML**
   - Paste code and save
3. Done!

## Mastery Levels

- **85%+**: Compliance Champion - Understands policies thoroughly
- **70%+**: Solid Compliance Knowledge - Understands most policies
- **50%+**: Developing Understanding - Learning our policies
- **<50%**: Additional Training Needed - Talk to manager

## Important Notes

**This is NOT a substitute for formal training.** Use this quiz:
- To reinforce compliance training
- To assess understanding of policies
- To identify areas that need discussion
- As a refresher on policies

Contact HR for questions about specific policies or situations.
